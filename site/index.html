Pluto

Train together.
Recover smarter.

Pluto turns your Fitbit Air data into three daily scores (Recovery, Sleep, and Effort), plus an Effort target for the day, sleep debt and a suggested bedtime, a biological age estimate, streaks, weekly challenges, and leaderboards with friends.

Private by design. Your raw health data stays on your iPhone. Only daily summaries are shared, and only with the groups and categories you choose.
Fair competition. Leaderboards rank effort, steps, and sleep. Recovery, HRV, and resting heart rate are never ranked.

Pluto reads data from the Google Health API with your permission. Scores are estimates to guide training, not medical advice.
