Getting started

Which Google account? Sign in with the Google account your Fitbit Air uses in the Google Health app. If Statline says the account isn't linked to Google Health, sign out and use that account instead.
"Google hasn't verified this app" Statline is a small independent app. If you see this screen, tap Advanced, then Go to Statline.
No data yet? Wear your Fitbit Air overnight and open the Google Health app so it syncs, then pull down to refresh in Statline. Recovery unlocks after 7 nights of HRV, and biological age after 14 days.
Joining friends Ask a friend for their group's invite code (or link), then enter it in the Team tab. You choose which categories each group can see.
Deleting your account Profile → Delete account removes your data from our server and your iPhone.

Scores, biological age, and bedtimes are estimates to guide habits, not medical advice.
